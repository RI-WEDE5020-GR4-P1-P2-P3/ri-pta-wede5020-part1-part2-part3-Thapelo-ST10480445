Who Can Receive Food Assistance?

Haven Community Food Bank serves the following community members:

  • Low-income families
  • Elderly residents (65+)
  • Persons with disabilities
  • Unemployed individuals

⚠️ Please bring an ID document for registration.

No appointment is needed. Just visit during distribution hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

Please bring your ID document for registration purposes.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment is needed. Just visit during distribution hours.

Can I pick up for someone else?

Yes, please bring their ID document and a note of authorisation.

What food items are available?

We provide nutritious groceries including fresh produce, non-perishable items, and hot meals.
